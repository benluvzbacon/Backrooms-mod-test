# The Backrooms (Fabric 1.21.1)

Put sand on your head and no-clip into the endless Backrooms.

## How to play

- **Getting in:** equip a sand block as a helmet — right-click the air with sand
  in hand, sneak + right-click a block, or place it via the creative inventory.
  One sand is consumed on entry (survival).
- **Level 0:** an infinite maze of yellow rooms, humming fluorescents, dead
  dark patches — and the **Still Life**, a weeping-angel mannequin in a tricorn
  hat. It only moves when nobody is looking. Don't blink.
- **Sanity:** drains in Level 0 (faster in darkness or near a Still Life),
  shown above the hotbar. Drink **Almond Water** (found in supply chests) to
  restore it.
- **The Poolrooms:** step into a lamp-ringed **pool portal** pad to reach the
  tiled maze of shallow pools. Every room is roofed, sanity slowly regenerates
  here, and rare tall **great halls** with window bands hide in the maze.
  Beware the fast 5-minute day/night cycle — pool water scalds during the day.
- Step into a Poolrooms portal pad to return to Level 0.

## Config

`config/backrooms.properties` — Still Life spawn rate/cap/range toggles plus
`normalSandOnly` (set to `false` to also accept red sand as the entry trigger).

## Building

```sh
./gradlew build        # mod jar lands in build/libs/
```

## Headless self-test

```sh
./gradlew runServer -Pselftest   # generates both dimensions, checks worldgen,
                                 # entities and mechanics, then exits 0/1
```

Art and sounds under `src/main/resources/assets/` are generated by
`tools/gen_textures.py`, `tools/gen_sounds.py` and `tools/gen_assets.py` —
re-running them must be a no-op (byte-identical output).
